# Pitch

**Women are under-diagnosed because their story arrives empty-handed.**

- Endometriosis takes 7 to 10 years on average to diagnose. PCOS goes undiagnosed in up to 70% of women who have it.
- A typical appointment is 15 minutes. The doctor sees a snapshot, not the 90 days that led there.
- So care starts at the standard protocol: a pill, a referral, "come back in three months."

**What is missing is the lifestyle and behavior record.** How she slept the week before her period, whether
her migraines cluster in the luteal phase, whether she has actually been eating enough protein, whether
stress spikes on days her calendar is stacked.

**Bloom builds that record without asking her to become a data-entry clerk.**
1. Passive: Oura ring sleep, HRV, temperature, activity flow in through a canonical adapter (any wearable next).
2. Effortless: one voice ramble a day, Claude sorts it into sleep, food, movement, mood, symptoms.
3. Motivating: cycle-aware daily plan, 3 habits picked by Claude from her 2 goals, one small win, and permission to ease off.
4. Useful to the doctor: a one-page record, by cycle phase, ready to share at the appointment.

**Why now**: wearables are mainstream among women 25 to 45, and LLMs can finally turn messy speech into structured health data.

**Ask**: pilot with OB-GYN and women's health clinics, where the record replaces the intake clipboard.
