# Demo script (2 minutes)

1. **Hook (15s)**: "She has had migraines for two years. Her doctor has 15 minutes and zero data."
2. **Onboard (25s)**: name, gym yes, vegetarian, migraines. Privacy page: "your record is yours." Pick 2 goals. Claude returns 3 habits.
3. **Today (25s)**: Oura connected. Cycle day and phase. Plan shifts with the phase. Scrub the cycle slider to late luteal: the "ease off today" card appears, the workout softens, the recipe changes.
4. **Log (25s)**: tap the mic, ramble: "slept badly, had oatmeal and a latte, 20 min walk, headache after lunch, feeling anxious." Watch it sort into cards.
5. **Mood check-in (10s)**: the bubble pops in. Tap a mood.
6. **Record (20s)**: the doctor summary. "After stressful days she sleeps 34 minutes less. Her steps halved this month. And her headaches cluster in the 5 days before her period, when sleep drops 21 minutes." Lifestyle first, cycle as one lens. (Read the live numbers off the Record tab; they come from backend/insights.py.) That is the cookie trail.
