# Demo script (3 minutes)

Source of truth: the speaker notes on the "demo" slide of the HerLife deck
(https://claude.ai/artifact/Eejcx6rXBV7ZjzprvGK47P). Framing: Medicine 3.0 and longevity. Daily behavior
compounds, and she walks into any appointment with the record of how her health has held up.

1. **Hook (20s)**: "She has had migraines for two years. Her doctor has 15 minutes and zero data."
2. **Sign in as Maya (15s)**: Sign in to a demo profile, then Maya (follicular, migraines, busy working woman).
3. **Today (35s)**: body weather, the 5 pillars, last night's REM and deep sleep forecast. Drag the cycle slider to late luteal: the "Ease off today" card appears. Tap "Book it" to drop a walk into a free calendar slot.
4. **Log (30s)**: Try sample 1, then Sort my log. The entries sort into Sleep, Movement, Nutrition, Mood, and Symptoms. The mood check-in follows.
5. **Dana, week 1 (30s)**: Switch, then Dana. She typed her goals in her own words; Claude chose what to track. Flags, the mountain wave of consistent days, and what moved since she started. This is the compounding beat.
6. **Maya's Record (30s)**: "After stressful days she sleeps 34 minutes less. Sunday nights run 51 minutes short. Her steps fell 46% this month. Headaches cluster before her period, when sleep drops 21 minutes." Tap "Write my doctor summary".
7. **Switch to Lena, 48 (20s)**: the cycle-change card fits the menopause transition, with a question to bring to her doctor. Under 40, HerLife never suggests menopause.

Route-arounds: if habits take long, tap "Use HerLife's picks instead". Don't use the live mic on stage. Don't claim the ring data belongs to a specific profile; Oura and the calendar are simulated in the demo build.
